# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business Sponsors

### Role Summary
Business Sponsors provide executive sponsorship and ensure that the project remains aligned with organizational strategy and investment priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic priority, funding, and high-level success measures
- Resolve escalated impediments that require executive authority
- Approve major scope, timeline, or resource changes
- Support stakeholder alignment and organizational adoption

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Receive status, risk, and escalation updates from Project Managers
- Align with Product Managers on outcomes, priorities, and trade-offs
- Provide direction and decisions to Project Managers while allowing Developers and delivery teams to own implementation details
- Confirm major decisions with affected Stakeholders

---

## Technical Leads and Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction and help the delivery team make sustainable design, integration, and quality decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate technical approaches and architecture constraints
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and non-functional requirements
- Facilitate technical decisions and document significant trade-offs
- Support estimation, sequencing, and readiness for implementation
- Promote maintainability, reliability, security, and observability

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Developers on design, implementation, reviews, and technical risks
- Work with Product Managers to assess feasibility and clarify technical implications of product choices
- Report technical dependencies and risks to Project Managers for planning and escalation
- Collaborate with QA/Testing on testability and non-functional validation
- Explain technical decisions to Stakeholders in terms of impact, risk, and outcomes

---

## UX and Product Designers

### Role Summary
UX and Product Designers represent user needs and shape usable, accessible experiences that support the desired product outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct or incorporate user research and identify usability needs
- Create workflows, prototypes, and interaction designs
- Define experience considerations and accessibility expectations
- Validate proposed solutions through usability feedback and testing
- Maintain design rationale and communicate changes to the delivery team

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with Product Managers to translate user needs into scope and acceptance criteria
- Work with Developers to explain designs, resolve implementation questions, and evaluate trade-offs
- Partner with QA/Testing to include usability, accessibility, and visual checks in validation
- Keep Project Managers informed about design dependencies and decisions
- Incorporate Stakeholder feedback without overriding agreed product priorities

---

## Release and DevOps Engineers

### Role Summary
Release and DevOps Engineers enable safe, repeatable delivery by managing deployment readiness, environments, observability, and rollback capability.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain deployment pipelines, environments, and release configuration
- Define operational readiness and rollback requirements
- Coordinate deployment windows and post-deployment verification
- Monitor releases and improve observability and incident response readiness
- Document release procedures, dependencies, and known operational risks

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers and Technical Leads on pipeline, infrastructure, and operational changes
- Partner with QA/Testing to verify release criteria and smoke-test coverage
- Coordinate timing, dependencies, and risks with Project Managers
- Provide Product Managers and Stakeholders with release-impact information and status
- Support Project Managers and Developers during rollback or incident response

---

## Customer Support and Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Operations Representatives bring customer and service-delivery insight into planning, readiness, and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Share recurring customer issues, operational constraints, and service trends
- Define support-readiness needs, training, and communication requirements
- Participate in incident triage and validate customer-impacting fixes
- Prepare support teams for releases and capture post-release feedback
- Feed operational learnings and unmet needs into the backlog

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with Product Managers to connect customer evidence to priorities and success measures
- Coordinate readiness plans and communications with Project Managers
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to reproduce issues and verify fixes
- Consult Technical Leads and Release/DevOps Engineers on operational impact and service reliability
- Provide Stakeholders with customer-impact updates during releases or incidents

---

## Security and Compliance Representatives

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Representatives help the team identify and satisfy security, privacy, regulatory, and policy requirements throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security, privacy, regulatory, and compliance requirements
- Review designs, controls, data handling, and operational procedures
- Track findings, required mitigations, and approval evidence
- Advise on risk acceptance and escalate material concerns
- Support security testing, release sign-off, and post-incident learning

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Engage Product Managers early to incorporate compliance requirements into scope and acceptance criteria
- Work with Technical Leads, Architects, and Developers on secure design and implementation
- Coordinate assessments and remediation tracking with Project Managers
- Partner with QA/Testing on security and compliance validation
- Advise Release/DevOps Engineers on deployment controls, secrets, monitoring, and rollback
- Communicate material risks and decisions to Stakeholders and Business Sponsors
